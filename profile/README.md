## Key Features of Internxt Drive

- **Zero-Knowledge Encryption** – Internxt Drive uses client-side encryption. Your data is encrypted before it ever leaves your device. Internxt Drive never sees your password or decryption keys.  
- **Open-Source Audibility** – Internxt Drive releases all client code on GitHub. Anyone can inspect how Internxt Drive handles encryption, sync, and authentication.  
- **Decentralized Storage** – Internxt Drive splits files into fragments and distributes them across multiple nodes. No single server holds a complete file. Internxt Drive prevents mass surveillance.  
- **Cross-Platform Sync** – Internxt Drive works on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS. Changes made on one device with Internxt Drive appear everywhere else within seconds.  
- **Encrypted Share Links** – Internxt Drive lets you create password-protected, expiring share links. Recipients can download files without needing an Internxt Drive account.  
- **Automatic Camera Uploads** – Internxt Drive mobile apps can auto-upload photos and videos to your private cloud. Internxt Drive organizes media by date and location metadata.  
- **File Version History** – Internxt Drive keeps previous versions of your files for up to 30 days (longer on paid plans). Restore accidentally overwritten documents directly inside Internxt Drive.  
- **Two-Factor Authentication** – Internxt Drive supports TOTP-based 2FA (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.). Even if someone steals your password, Internxt Drive remains locked.  
- **Command-Line Interface** – Internxt Drive offers a CLI tool for scripting uploads, syncs, and remote management. Automate backups with Internxt Drive using cron jobs or Task Scheduler.  
- **Biometric Login** – Internxt Drive mobile apps support Face ID and fingerprint unlocking. Access your encrypted files on Internxt Drive with one touch.

## Quick Start with Internxt Drive

1. **Get Internxt Drive** – Visit the official website or download the portable version (no installation required for Windows). Internxt Drive is also available on Snap Store (Linux) and App Store (macOS).  
2. **Launch** – Double-click `Internxt Drive` icon. The main window opens in under 2 seconds on SSD drives. Internxt Drive checks for updates automatically.  
3. **Create your account** – Click "Sign Up" inside Internxt Drive. Use a strong, unique password — Internxt Drive will never store it. Verify your email.  
4. **Start syncing** – Drag any folder into Internxt Drive. The app shows real-time encryption progress. Internxt Drive creates a virtual drive letter (Windows) or a mount point (Linux).  
5. **Share securely** – Right-click any file inside Internxt Drive → "Create share link". Set a password and expiration date. Internxt Drive generates an encrypted URL instantly.  
6. **Adjust (optional)** – Change sync intervals, bandwidth limits, and startup behavior. Most users leave Internxt Drive on default settings for optimal privacy-throughput balance.  
7. **Work normally** – Internxt Drive runs in your system tray. You forget it’s there — until you need to recover a previous version or share a 10 GB file safely.
